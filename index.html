How to Play Dol

Goal: First team to 21 points wins.


Teams: You & Ally vs Opp Left & Opp Right.


Dol vs Inn: A trick with a 10 is a Dol. Without a 10 it's an Inn.


Scoring: 4 tens = 5pts · 3 tens = 4pts/1pt · 2 each = 3pts/2pts by Inn count.


Rules: Follow the lead suit. If void, play anything — including trump. Trump beats all other suits.
